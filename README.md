#CONCEPT & IDEA: 
Building traffic lights using electronics involves combining components like LEDs, resistors, microcontrollers (like Arduino), and control circuits.
Components Required:
LEDs: Represent different traffic light states (red, yellow, green).

Resistors: Limit current to prevent LEDs from burning out.

Microcontroller: Arduino Uno to control the sequence.

Breadboard: For assembling the circuit without soldering.

Push Button: For pedestrian crossing.

Power Supply: Usually from the Arduino's 5V output.
Pedestrian Signals: Include a button that pedestrians can press to request a walk signal.
Traffic lights play a crucial role in maintaining order and safety on the roads. They are a prime example of how simple, yet effective, engineering solutions can have a significant impact on daily life
